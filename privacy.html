Privacy Policy

SciFacts Publisher · Last updated: November 16, 2026

SciFacts Publisher ("the tool") is an internal publishing tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel @ablaihangalimov5131. The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The tool has no public sign-up and no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's Google account, with the scopes needed to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channel (youtube.upload, youtube).
  • The owner's channel ID and channel title, read to confirm that uploads go to the right channel.
  • Metadata of videos the tool uploads (video ID, title, privacy status), saved as a record of what was published.

The tool does not access, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers, comments or any other YouTube users.

How the data is used

Solely to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel and to keep a log of those uploads. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

The OAuth token is stored on the owner's computer and, for scheduled runs, in the owner's private source repository encrypted with AES-256; the decryption key is kept separately in the owner's private cloud environment settings. Upload records (video IDs and titles) are stored in the same private repository.

Sharing

The tool does not sell, share or disclose any data obtained through YouTube API Services to third parties.

Cookies

The tool is a command-line program; it does not place cookies or similar technologies on users' devices.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, stored tokens stop working. Stored tokens and upload records can be deleted on request; requests are handled within 7 days.
